NOTTING HILL CARNIVAL 2026 - INTERACTIVE MAP & NAVIGATOR
DOCUMENTATION & FEATURE SPECIFICATION

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OVERVIEW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Notting Hill Carnival 2026 Interactive Map & Navigator is a lightweight,
high-performance web application designed for carnival attendees, visitors, 
and engineering teams. It provides real-time geographic navigation across 
37 sound systems, 40 toilet stations, 11 tube stations, first aid posts, 
food zones, and official parade routes.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TFL TRAVEL ALERTS TICKER FEATURE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Global Header Placement: Positioned at the top of the app viewport across both
  desktop and mobile views.
- Continuous Marquee: Uses a dual-track CSS keyframe animation for a seamless 
  infinite loop with zero blank gaps or pauses.
- Interactive Station Jump: Each alert inside the ticker is linked to its
  respective tube station marker. Clicking any alert text (e.g., Westbourne 
  Park, Holland Park, Ladbroke Grove) smoothly flies the map directly to that 
  station and opens its status popup.
- Alerts Included:
  * ⛔ Ladbroke Grove Station: CLOSED ALL WEEKEND (Circle / Hammersmith & City)
  * ⚠️ Westbourne Park Station: EXIT ONLY from 11:00 AM (No Entry)
  * ⚠️ Holland Park Station: Often closes early (~15:00)
  * ℹ️ Recommended Alternatives: Bayswater & Queensway Stations
- Dismiss Toggle: Includes a clean close button (✕) to hide the banner.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. SOUND SYSTEMS & GENRE FILTERING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 37 Sound Systems: Includes exact GPS coordinates, full genre tags, and stage
  details for all official carnival sound systems (e.g., Mastermind, Aba Shanti,
  Channel One, Rampage, Saxon Studio, Gaz's Rockin' Blues, etc.).
- Quick Preset Filter Pills:
  * [All]: Displays all 37 systems.
  * [⭐ Favorites]: Shows only starred sound systems.
  * [🇯🇲 Reggae & Dub]: Filters for Reggae, Dub, Lovers Rock, and Steppers.
  * [💃 Soca & Calypso]: Filters for Soca, Calypso, and Caribbean Party Music.
  * [🎧 House & Techno]: Filters for House, Tech House, Techno, and Electronic.
  * [🎤 Hip-Hop & R&B]: Filters for Hip-Hop, R&B, Garage, Urban, and Soul.
- Real-Time Search Box: Instant search by system name or genre with a clear 
  button (✕).
- Individual Genre Chips: Select single or multiple genre tags simultaneously.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FAVORITES SYSTEM (⭐)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Star buttons (⭐) embedded on each sidebar list card and inside map popups.
- Persistent Storage: Saved in browser `localStorage` under key `nhc26_favs`.
- Filter View: Instant access to all favorited stages via the "⭐ Favorites" 
  preset button.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. REAL-TIME GPS GEOLOCATION (📍)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Floating Map Control: "📍 Find My Location" button on top-right map controls.
- Uses Leaflet's `map.locate()` with high-accuracy GPS positioning.
- Displays a pulsating blue user marker and accuracy radius circle.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. BASEMAP & DARK MODE SWITCHER
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dark Theme Default: CartoDB Dark Matter tile layer matching carnival night vibe.
- Light Theme Toggle: Switch between Dark Map and standard OpenStreetMap tiles.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. FACILITY & ROUTE LAYERS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 40 Public Toilets (WC): Blue pin markers with turn-by-turn directions.
- 8 First Aid Posts: Green cross markers with medical team info.
- 5 Welfare Points: Pink heart markers for lost children & welfare support.
- 19 Food & Trading Zones: Orange fork-and-knife pins for licensed food stalls.
- 11 Tube & Rail Stations: Custom UK Underground roundel pins with real-time status.
- Official Parade Route: Red polyline circuit with directional chevrons (>>).
- Perimeter Boundary: Dashed gold polygon representing official carnival zone.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. WALKING NAVIGATION SHORTCUTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every map popup includes 1-click turn-by-turn walking directions links:
- Google Maps (destination coordinates)
- Apple Maps (walking mode parameter)
- Citymapper (destination name & coordinates)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. MOBILE RESPONSIVE DRAWER VIEW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Tab switcher bar for mobile devices (<=768px):
  * [🗺️ Map View]: Full screen map navigation.
  * [📋 Filters & Systems (37)]: Collapsible drawer list & filters.
- Selecting any sound system card automatically switches to Map View and flies
  to the stage location.
